How to delete your data

{{PRODUCT}} makes short Instagram Reels for insurance agents. You can download everything we store about you, and ask for all of it to be deleted, at any time.

If you have an account

  1. Log in, then open Account.
  2. Under Your data, tap Download my data if you want a copy first. It’s one file with your settings, your video list and your approval records.
  3. Tap Request deletion, tick the box and confirm. We email you to say we got it.

We then delete your workspace and everything in it, and email you when it’s done. You can cancel the request from Account until then.

If you can’t log in

Please {{CONTACT}} and ask for your data to be deleted. Write from the email address on your account so we can find it.

Instagram

If you connected Instagram, you can remove our access on your own at any time:

  • In the app: Setup → Autopilot → Instagram → Disconnect. This deletes the access token we hold straight away.
  • In Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites, then remove {{PRODUCT}}.

Reels you already posted belong to your Instagram account. Deleting your data here doesn’t remove them from Instagram; delete them in Instagram if you want them gone.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and anyone else’s login in your workspace.
  • Your settings, facts and their saved versions.
  • Your videos, covers, logo and the clips you uploaded.
  • Your approval records, coach notes and usage counts.
  • Your Instagram connection.

Our nightly backups still hold a copy until they’re replaced, within {{BACKUP_DAYS}} days. Our payment provider (Stripe) keeps its own records of payments, as the law requires. A security log of logins and account actions (who did what and when, with the IP address) is kept for up to {{AUDIT_MONTHS}} months to protect accounts and investigate misuse, then deleted.

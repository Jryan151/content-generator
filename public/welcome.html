Questions

Why invite only?

We add agents a few at a time so everyone gets set up properly. Request access below and we’ll be in touch.

Will my carrier accept these videos?

They’re compliance-checked: the app blocks the risky wording it can detect and only uses facts you confirmed. Many carriers and states still want to review ads first. You approve every video and stay responsible for it, and your approval records help with those reviews.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Videos use stock footage with captions, or captions plus an AI voiceover (the post says it’s an AI voice). You can add your logo to the end card.

Which products does it cover?

Final expense, mortgage protection and family protection.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from Billing. You can also download all your data, and ask us to delete it.
